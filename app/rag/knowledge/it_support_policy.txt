Enterprise IT Support Policy

Password Reset

Employees can request a password reset through the IT support portal.
Password reset requests are normally processed within 30 minutes.

Laptop Issues

Employees experiencing laptop hardware problems should create an IT
support ticket. Critical hardware issues should be marked as high priority.

VPN Access

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
from outside the corporate network.

Support Hours

Standard IT support is available Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM.
